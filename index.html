Volume and net revenue come from Grail Break's platform numbers and include $BREAK trading fees claimed by Grail Break, not only product fees. Packs opened, card value pulled, users, breaks and the buyback ledger come from the same source; buybacks are valued in USD at the time of each burn, and the share of supply burned is measured against $BREAK's original 1B supply. "Returned as cards", volume per buyer and the ARPU figures are this tracker's own arithmetic on those numbers. Prices and market caps come from DexScreener; Collector Crypt's 30-day pack sales and revenue come from DefiLlama. Data refreshes every 5 minutes.
